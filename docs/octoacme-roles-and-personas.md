# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead day-to-day delivery execution, team health, and technical planning. They turn product direction into actionable engineering work and help remove blockers that slow the team down.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, resourcing, and work distribution
- Support engineering planning, sprint execution, and dependency management
- Coach engineers and help improve delivery quality and consistency
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on trade-offs and sequencing
- Escalate delivery risks and resolve cross-team blockers when needed

### Goals
- Maintain predictable, sustainable delivery
- Improve team health, focus, and throughput
- Ensure execution is aligned with product priorities and quality expectations

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and engineering reviews
- Team health and staffing updates
- Dependency and risk escalation discussions with PMs and product leadership

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, validation plans, and release-readiness criteria. They help ensure that software meets the expected user experience and business requirements before it reaches production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage expectations, and release gates
- Coordinate manual QA, automated validation, and regression checks
- Partner with developers to review edge cases and acceptance criteria
- Escalate quality risks and production readiness concerns to PMs and stakeholders
- Help ensure high-confidence delivery across sprint and release milestones

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and quality surprises
- Improve confidence in delivery readiness
- Align testing with user value and acceptance standards

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and quality reviews
- Release-readiness checkpoints with engineering and PMs
- Defect triage and risk updates during delivery cycles

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization from operational, technical, and policy risks. They guide secure design decisions, compliance requirements, and incident-response coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risk exposure and identify security or compliance requirements
- Provide guidance on secure architecture, data handling, and access controls
- Coordinate with engineering teams during design, implementation, and deployment
- Support incident response and post-incident learning for security-related issues
- Ensure governance standards are documented and followed across the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Build trust in product and operational decisions
- Support safe, resilient delivery of customer-facing and internal systems

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and risk assessments
- Compliance checkpoints and remediation planning
- Escalations during incidents or critical vulnerabilities

---

## Release Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, release sequencing, and communication around production changes. They help keep releases predictable and aligned with stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows, milestones, and rollback readiness
- Confirm deployment checks, smoke tests, and stakeholder communications are complete
- Coordinate with engineering, QA, and PMs on launch readiness
- Monitor production change impact and help manage release risk
- Document deployment outcomes and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Reduce release instability and operational surprises
- Improve communication before, during, and after launches
- Keep stakeholder expectations aligned with delivery realities

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Deployment coordination across engineering and support teams
- Post-release feedback and issue escalation communication

---

## Customer Success / Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Leads translate customer feedback, adoption issues, and support signals into actionable product or delivery improvements. They help ensure customer needs remain visible throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Capture product issues, support trends, and customer feedback
- Collaborate with Product Managers on priority-setting and customer impact
- Surface recurring blockers, usage pain points, and adoption risks
- Coordinate with engineering and PM teams on customer-impacting fixes and escalations
- Help communicate known issues, mitigations, and product updates to stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure customer feedback informs delivery and prioritization
- Reduce friction between product teams and customer-facing teams

### Typical Communication
- Customer issue tracking and feedback reviews
- Support escalation summaries and adoption updates
- Cross-functional coordination on fixes and customer communications

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define success metrics, monitor outcomes, and help the team make evidence-based decisions. They connect product execution to measurable business and user impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, product health indicators, and reporting baselines
- Partner with product and engineering teams to instrument features and events
- Review operational, product, and business telemetry for decision-making
- Help assess whether initiatives are delivering expected outcomes
- Support stakeholder reporting with clear, measurable summaries

### Goals
- Improve evidence-based decision-making
- Make impact visible and measurable
- Align team execution with business and customer outcomes

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and product performance reporting
- Insights sharing with PMs, product leaders, and stakeholders
- Decision support for roadmap and release prioritization

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles provide clearer ownership boundaries, escalation paths, and collaboration patterns across planning, execution, quality, release, and stakeholder communication.
